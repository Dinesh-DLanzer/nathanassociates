Disciplined Engineering Sequence

Every site engagement follows a strict four-stage technical protocol so project decisions rest on verified measurements.

Integrated Field Survey & Drone Mapping — Tamil Nadu
Stage One

Field Understanding

We examine title records, village survey sketches (FMB), site topography, and client project milestones before deploying field crews.

Stage Two

Site Measurement

Trained surveyor crews deploy dual-frequency DGPS instruments, electronic total stations, and UAVs to log true positional coordinates.

Stage Three

Technical Synthesis

Raw coordinates are verified against cadastral records, processed into CAD layers, contour maps, and formal authority drawings.

Stage Four

Certified Delivery

Delivery of stamped documentation, digital coordinate schedules, and durable physical boundary stones ready for civil execution.
